How the Backend Processes SMS Replies

📲
Renter receives this SMS: "StorageDefender: Motion detected in Unit 980 at Mason Facility on Jun 18 8:13 PM. Was this you? Reply Yes/No"
The renter's reply goes to Twilio → RabbitMQ → alert_interactor.ex → alert.ex where it's matched against the keyword lists using a regex pattern.

Backend Regex Pattern

~r/^\s*[[:punct:]]*\s*(keyword)\s*[[:punct:]]*\s*$/i
  • Case-insensitive (YES = yes = Yes)
  • Allows punctuation edges — "Yes!" works
  • Keyword must be the only word
  • "Yes please" → ❌ FAILS (extra word)
  • "it was me" → ❌ FAILS (multi-word)
Three possible outcomes for every reply:
Backend Result Alert Status What happens to the incident SMS sent back to renter
:claim_accept :confirmed Incident resolved — presence_confirmed "Thank you, this notification was cleared!"
:claim_deny :denied Incident escalated — facility manager notified, Slack alert "Thanks for confirming, please contact [Facility] during office hours"
:claim_unparseable :bad_response Incident stays open — nothing escalated, nothing cleared "Hi [FirstName], thanks for your reply! If you need help with Unit [#], please contact the facility at [Phone]"

Renter Impact Summary

🏪
Who are renters? Storage unit customers at facilities using Storage Defender. They receive SMS motion alerts when their unit triggers a sensor during access hours. They reply to confirm or deny the motion. Many are not tech-savvy and will reply with whatever feels natural.
Scenario Renter replies System sees Real-world impact on renter
Renter was in their unit, replies naturally correct or ok :claim_unparseable Renter thinks they responded. Facility manager still gets an alert. Potential unnecessary contact / visit to the unit. Renter is confused why they're still being contacted.
Renter types single letter (common on mobile) y or n :claim_unparseable For "y": motion not cleared, facility alerted unnecessarily.
For "n": break-in NOT reported — safety risk.
Renter wasn't in unit, replies naturally not me or wasn't me :claim_unparseable Potential break-in is NOT escalated to facility manager. Security risk — the facility is never alerted even though the renter clearly communicated denial.
Renter confirms with "confirmed" confirmed :claim_unparseable Business/professional renters often use this word. Motion not cleared — facility gets an alert for a legitimate access.
Renter replies with sentence yes it was me :claim_unparseable Even though the message clearly starts with "yes", regex requires single word only. Incident stays open.
Renter uses any recognized word yes yeah yep etc. :claim_accept ✅ Incident cleared correctly. No follow-up needed.
Renter uses recognized NO word no nope etc. :claim_deny ✅ Incident escalated correctly. Facility manager notified. Security response triggered.
